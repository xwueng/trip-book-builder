What a trip book is

A small book made from your trip photos. You flip the pages on a phone or computer, like a real book. Claude AI builds it for you.

Golden Gate Bridge June 14, 2026 Windy walk across at sunset. We watched the fog roll in. The orange color was first used as a primer and kept because it shows up in fog.
  • One page for each photo.
  • Each page has the place, the date, your words and a short fact.
  • A cover at the start. A map of your trip at the end.
  • Share it with family as a link or a PDF.

Want to make one? Read the Student Handbook.

Common questions

Do I have to write the words for each photo?

No. You can tell Claude what you remember, or skip and let Claude write it for you.

What if I don't remember where a photo was taken?

Claude makes a best guess and marks it “confirm venue” so you can check it.

Can family on an iPhone or iPad see it?

Yes. The link opens in any browser, and the pages still turn. A very large book can be made into a PDF instead.

Who can see my book?

Only you, until you choose to share it. Anyone you send the link to can see it.

What device do I need?

A phone is needed either way. A phone plus a laptop works best: read the handbook on one, work in Claude on the other.
